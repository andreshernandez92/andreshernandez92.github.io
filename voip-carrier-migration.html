Helping move a live contact center's phone numbers to a new carrier: supporting the number porting and building the call flow the numbers land on.

The problem

The contact center's phone numbers were with iPFone and were being moved to DIDWW. Each number fed a queue or an IVR, so if a number finished porting before its routing was ready, callers would hear an error instead of reaching an agent.

Objective: Get the numbers ported to DIDWW and make sure each one reached the correct destination through a working call flow as soon as it went live.

Step by step

1

Organize the numbers to be moved

  • Helped put together the list of numbers being ported and what each one was used for.
  • Matched each number to the queue, IVR or team it needed to reach once it was on DIDWW.

Best practice: one list, one source of truth

Keep a single sheet with every number, its purpose, its destination and its porting status. Everyone on the migration works from the same list.

2

Support the number porting

  • Helped gather the information the port requests needed and follow the requests through to completion.
  • Tracked which numbers had completed porting and which were still pending.

Lesson: details must match the old carrier exactly

Port requests are rejected when the account details differ even slightly from what the losing carrier has on file. Check them against a recent bill before submitting.

3

Build the inbound call flow

  • Created the call flow the ported numbers route into: which queue or IVR each number reaches, and what happens when nobody answers or it is after hours.
  • Set it up before the numbers went live, so they worked the moment porting completed.

Best practice: route before you port

If a number activates before its routing exists, callers get an error until someone notices. Build the flow first, then let the number go live.

4

Test and verify

  • Placed test calls through the flow to confirm each path reached the right place, including menu options and after-hours handling.
  • Called the ported numbers after they went live to confirm they routed correctly.

Outcome

  • Numbers moved from iPFone to DIDWW with a call flow ready for them.
  • Each number tested after porting to confirm it reached the right destination.
  • A clear number list documenting where every line goes.
